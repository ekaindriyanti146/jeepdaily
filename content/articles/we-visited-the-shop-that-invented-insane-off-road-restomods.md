### FAQ

1.

**What is an off-road restomod?**
An off-road restomod is a vintage vehicle that has been modified for off-road use, often with modern engines, suspension systems, and other components.
2.

**How much does an off-road restomod cost?**
The cost of an off-road restomod can vary widely, depending on the vehicle, the extent of the modifications, and the shop performing the work.

Prices can range from $50,000 to $200,000 or more.
3.

**Can I build my own off-road restomod?**
Yes, it is possible to build your own off-road restomod, but it requires a significant amount of time, money, and expertise.

It's often recommended to work with a professional shop to ensure that the vehicle is safe and performs well.
4.

**Are off-road restomods reliable?**
Off-road restomods can be reliable, but it depends on the quality of the build and the components used.

It's essential to work with a reputable shop and to regularly maintain the vehicle to ensure its reliability and performance.
