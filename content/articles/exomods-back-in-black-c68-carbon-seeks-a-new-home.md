## Hook

The world of bespoke automotive creations is replete with examples of stunning vehicles that push the boundaries of design, performance, and innovation.

Among these, EXOMOD Concepts' 'Back In Black' C68 Carbon stands out as a testament to human ingenuity and the relentless pursuit of perfection.

This vehicle, born from a 2023 Dodge Challenger SRT HELLCAT Widebody Jailbreak, has been transformed into a carbon-fiber masterpiece that is as much a work of art as it is a high-performance machine.

With its return to BringATrailer, the 'Back In Black' C68 Carbon seeks a new home, offering a unique opportunity for collectors and enthusiasts to own a piece of automotive history.

## Section 1: History/Context

The 'Back In Black' C68 Carbon is part of EXOMOD Concepts' lineage of creating extraordinary vehicles from already impressive bases.

The 2023 Dodge Challenger SRT HELLCAT Widebody Jailbreak, being from the final 'Last Call' model year, already boasts significant credentials.

The result is a vehicle that is both a tribute to the original Dodge Challenger and a bold statement of innovation and design.

## Section 2: Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

At the heart of the 'Back In Black' C68 Carbon is the powerful 6.2L Supercharged V8 engine, capable of producing 807 horsepower.

This engine is paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, providing smooth and rapid gear shifts.

The vehicle's suspension has been tuned for optimal performance, combining the benefits of a lowered stance with the capability to absorb road irregularities.

The extensive use of carbon fiber in the body panels, including the hood, trunk lid, and roof, significantly reduces the vehicle's weight, improving power-to-weight ratio and handling.

## Section 3: Real World Performance

The 'Back In Black' C68 Carbon is designed to deliver exceptional real-world performance. With its powerful engine and lightweight body, it can accelerate from 0-60 mph in under 3.5 seconds. The handling is precise and responsive, thanks to the tuned suspension and high-performance tires. Whether on the track or the street, this vehicle is capable of providing an exhilarating driving experience. The extensive use of carbon fiber not only enhances performance but also adds a layer of exclusivity and uniqueness to the vehicle.

## Section 4: Comparison/Verdict

In comparison to other bespoke vehicles in the market, the 'Back In Black' C68 Carbon stands out due to its meticulous attention to detail, the quality of its carbon fiber construction, and its underlying performance capabilities. It represents a rare opportunity for someone to own a truly one-of-a-kind vehicle that combines the best of American muscle with the sophistication of Italian design.
