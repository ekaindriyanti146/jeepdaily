## Section 2: Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

Under the hood of the 2028 Jeep Scrambler SRT lies a potent 6.4-liter V8 engine, producing a whopping 475 horsepower and 470 lb-ft of torque.

This engine is paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, which provides smooth and seamless shifting.

The suspension system has been upgraded to include a high-performance coil-over shock setup, complete with adjustable damping and ride height.

This allows drivers to fine-tune their suspension to suit their specific needs, whether that's tackling treacherous off-road terrain or carving up twisty roads.

The following table highlights some of the key technical specifications of the 2028 Jeep Scrambler SRT:
| Feature | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Engine | 6.4-liter V8 |

| Torque | 470 lb-ft |
| Transmission | 8-speed automatic |
| Suspension | High-performance coil-over shock setup |
| Ground Clearance | 11.5 inches |
| Approach Angle | 35.5 degrees |
| Departure Angle | 29.5 degrees |
| Breakover Angle | 23.5 degrees |
