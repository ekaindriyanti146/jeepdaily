## Section 2: Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

The 2021 RED Jeep Wrangler Unlimited Night Eagle Hardtop is powered by a 3.6-liter V6 engine, producing 285 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque. The engine is paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, providing smooth and responsive shifting. The suspension system features a heavy-duty Dana 30 front axle and a Dana 35 rear axle, with a five-link coil suspension setup. This combination provides exceptional articulation, stability, and control, making it ideal for off-road adventures. Other notable features include:

| Feature | Description |

| Suspension | Five-link coil suspension |
| Axles | Dana 30 front, Dana 35 rear |
