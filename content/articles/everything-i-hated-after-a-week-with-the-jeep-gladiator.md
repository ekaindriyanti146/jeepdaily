## Hook

The Jeep Gladiator Willys is a name that resonates deeply within the off-road community, evoking images of rugged terrain, unforgiving landscapes, and the unrelenting pursuit of adventure.

As a senior automotive journalist, I had the unique opportunity to spend a week behind the wheel of this iconic vehicle, putting it through its paces on both paved roads and the untamed wilderness.

My experience was a mixed bag, filled with moments of awe-inspiring capability and frustrating shortcomings.

In this review, we'll delve into the details of what I hated about the Jeep Gladiator Willys after a week of intense testing.

## Section 1: History/Context

The Jeep Gladiator Willys is a modern interpretation of the classic Willys Jeep, a vehicle that has been synonymous with off-road prowess and durability for decades.

The Gladiator itself is a pickup truck version of the Jeep Wrangler, designed to combine the versatility of a truck bed with the go-anywhere capability of a Wrangler.

The Willys edition is a specific trim level that pays homage to the original Willys Jeep, with unique styling cues and off-road enhancements.

## Section 2: Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

Technically, the Jeep Gladiator Willys is equipped with a 3.6-liter V6 engine, producing 285 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque.

This powerplant is paired with a six-speed manual transmission, although an eight-speed automatic is also available.

The suspension system features a five-link coil setup in the front and a five-link leaf spring design in the rear, providing a balance between on-road comfort and off-road articulation.

The Willys edition comes with 32-inch tires and a limited-slip rear differential as standard, enhancing its off-road credentials.

The following table summarizes the key technical specifications of the Jeep Gladiator Willys:

| Specification | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Engine | 3.6-liter V6 |
| Horsepower | 285 hp |
| Torque | 260 lb-ft |
| Transmission | 6-speed manual or 8-speed automatic |
| Suspension (Front) | Five-link coil |
| Suspension (Rear) | Five-link leaf spring |
| Tires | 32-inch |
| Differential (Rear) | Limited-slip |

## Section 3: Real World Performance

In real-world testing, the Jeep Gladiator Willys proved to be a formidable off-road performer, capable of tackling challenging terrain with ease. The combination of its robust engine, capable suspension, and aggressive tire tread pattern allowed it to traverse rocky inclines, sandy dunes, and muddy trails with confidence. However, on paved roads, the experience was less polished, with noticeable vibration and noise from the tires, and a somewhat disconnected driving feel due to the vehicle's tall stance and soft suspension.

## Section 4: Comparison/Verdict

Comparing the Jeep Gladiator Willys to its competitors in the off-road pickup truck segment, such as the Ford Ranger and the Toyota Tacoma, it's clear that the Gladiator Willys occupies a unique niche.

Its unparalleled off-road capability, coupled with its nostalgic appeal and rugged styling, make it a compelling choice for enthusiasts.

However, its on-road manners and interior amenities may not match those of more road-oriented pickups.

Ultimately, the decision to purchase a Jeep Gladiator Willys depends on one's priorities: for those who value off-road adventure above all else, the Willys is an excellent choice.

For others seeking a more balanced on-road and off-road experience, alternative options may be more suitable.

### FAQ

1. **What is the Jeep Gladiator Willys?**

- The Jeep Gladiator Willys is a special edition of the Jeep Gladiator pickup truck, designed to pay homage to the classic Willys Jeep with unique styling and off-road enhancements.

2. **What engine does the Jeep Gladiator Willys come with?**

- The Jeep Gladiator Willys is equipped with a 3.6-liter V6 engine, producing 285 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque.

3. **Is the Jeep Gladiator Willys good for off-road driving?**

- Yes, the Jeep Gladiator Willys is highly capable off-road, thanks to its robust engine, capable suspension, aggressive tires, and limited-slip rear differential.

4. **How does the Jeep Gladiator Willys perform on paved roads?**

- On paved roads, the Jeep Gladiator Willys exhibits noticeable vibration and noise from the tires, and a somewhat disconnected driving feel, making it less refined than some of its competitors for on-road driving.
