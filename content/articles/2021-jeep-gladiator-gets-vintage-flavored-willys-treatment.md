The Gladiator Willys Edition also features a Command-Trac part-time four-wheel-drive system, which provides exceptional traction and control on a variety of terrain.

The suspension system on the Gladiator Willys Edition is designed to provide a smooth ride on paved roads, while still being capable of handling rugged off-road terrain. The front suspension features a five-link coil design, while the rear suspension uses a five-link coil design with a stabilizer bar. This suspension system, combined with the Gladiator's rugged body-on-frame design, provides exceptional stability and control on a variety of surfaces.
