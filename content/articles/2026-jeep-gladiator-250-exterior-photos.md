## Section 2: Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

The Gladiator also features a range of transmission options, including a six-speed manual and an eight-speed automatic.

The suspension system is equally impressive, with a five-link coil suspension in the front and a five-link leaf spring suspension in the rear.

This combination provides a smooth and stable ride, both on and off the road.
