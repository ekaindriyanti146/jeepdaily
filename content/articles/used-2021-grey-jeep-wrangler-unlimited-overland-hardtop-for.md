## Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

The 2021 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited Overland Hardtop is powered by a 3.6-liter V6 engine that produces 285 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque. This engine is paired with an eight-speed automatic transmission and a four-wheel-drive system that features a two-speed transfer case. The Wrangler also features a robust suspension system that includes a five-link coil suspension in the front and a five-link coil suspension with a track bar in the rear. The vehicle's off-road capabilities are further enhanced by its high ground clearance, approach and departure angles, and water fording depth.

## Real World Performance

The 2021 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited Overland Hardtop is a capable off-road vehicle that can handle a wide range of terrain, from rocky trails to sandy dunes. The vehicle's four-wheel-drive system and high ground clearance make it well-suited for navigating challenging off-road terrain, while its advanced safety features, including electronic stability control and hill descent control, help to ensure a safe and stable ride. On the road, the Wrangler is comfortable and composed, with a smooth ride and responsive handling.
