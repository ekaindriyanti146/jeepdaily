The Dodge Charger has a long and storied history, particularly in its role as a police vehicle. Its power, durability, and spacious interior have made it a favorite among law enforcement agencies. The revelation at the Police Fleet Expo 2026 not only confirms the Charger's return but also highlights Dodge's commitment to serving the needs of law enforcement.

The Police Fleet Expo itself is a critical event where manufacturers and law enforcement agencies come together to discuss the latest in police vehicles and technology. It serves as a platform for manufacturers to showcase their capabilities and for law enforcement to express their needs and challenges. The introduction of the next-generation Dodge Charger police sedan at this expo underscores the importance of this event and the role of the Charger in the law enforcement community.
