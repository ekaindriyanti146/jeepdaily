The Ford Everest has its roots in the early 2000s, emerging as a response to the growing demand for SUVs that could handle both the urban jungle and the great outdoors with ease.

At the heart of the 2026 Ford Everest Active lies a potent 3.0L V6 diesel engine, capable of producing 184kW of power and 600Nm of torque.

This is paired with a smooth-shifting 8-speed automatic transmission, designed to optimize power delivery and fuel efficiency.

The Everest Active boasts a sophisticated four-wheel-drive system, complete with a two-speed electronic transfer case and a rear differential lock, allowing for superior traction and control in challenging off-road conditions.

The suspension system has been tuned for both on-road comfort and off-road articulation, featuring a coil-spring independent front suspension and a watt's link rear suspension.

## Section 3: Real World Performance

In real-world testing, the 2026 Ford Everest Active demonstrates its capability to handle a variety of terrains with ease.

Whether navigating through dense city traffic or tackling steep mountain trails, the vehicle exhibits a commendable blend of power, agility, and stability.

The advanced four-wheel-drive system and the generous ground clearance of 251mm allow the Everest Active to traverse challenging off-road routes, including rocky inclines, sandy dunes, and muddy bogs, with confidence.

On the road, the Everest Active offers a comfortable and quiet ride, with the suspension effectively absorbing bumps and irregularities, making it an ideal choice for both daily commuting and long-distance travel.

## Section 4: Comparison/Verdict

When compared to its competitors in the off-road SUV segment, the 2026 Ford Everest Active stands out for its well-rounded package of performance, capability, and features.

While vehicles like the Toyota Prado and the Isuzu MU-X offer strong competition, the Everest Active's refined on-road manners, coupled with its impressive off-road credentials, make it a compelling option for those seeking a vehicle that can seamlessly transition between urban and wilderness environments.

In conclusion, the 2026 Ford Everest Active is a testament to Ford's commitment to creating vehicles that are as capable as they are comfortable, making it a top choice for anyone in the market for a versatile and reliable off-road companion.

### FAQ

1. **What is the towing capacity of the 2026 Ford Everest Active?**

- The 2026 Ford Everest Active has a maximum towing capacity of up to 3100kg, depending on the specific configuration and towing setup.

2. **Does the 2026 Ford Everest Active come with advanced safety features?**

- Yes, the 2026 Ford Everest Active is equipped with a comprehensive suite of safety features, including adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping aid, and a 360-degree camera system.

3. **Can the 2026 Ford Everest Active handle water crossings?**

- The 2026 Ford Everest Active has a wading depth of up to 800mm, allowing it to navigate through water crossings with caution, provided that the water is not moving too swiftly and the vehicle's approach and departure angles are not compromised.

4. **Is the 2026 Ford Everest Active available in various trim levels?**

- Yes, the 2026 Ford Everest is available in several trim levels, including the Active, Sport, and Titanium, each offering a unique combination of features, technologies, and styling elements to cater to different preferences and needs.
