## Section 3: Real World Performance

The real-world performance of Nissan's off-road EV would have been a significant factor in its success or failure.

The vehicle would have needed to balance on-road comfort with off-road capability, a challenging task for any manufacturer.

The electric powertrain would have provided instant torque, making the vehicle well-suited for rock crawling and other low-speed off-road activities.

However, the vehicle's weight, ground clearance, and approach/departure angles would have been critical in determining its overall off-road prowess.

Nissan's decision to abandon the project may indicate that the company was unable to achieve the desired level of performance, or that the costs associated with developing such a vehicle were deemed too high.

## Section 4: Comparison/Verdict

In comparison to other off-road EVs on the market, Nissan's abandoned project would have faced significant competition.
