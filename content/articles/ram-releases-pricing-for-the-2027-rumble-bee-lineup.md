The next tier offers a 6.4-liter V8, generating 485 horsepower and 475 lb-ft of torque.

For those seeking even more power, the Rumble Bee lineup includes a 6.2-liter supercharged V8, churning out 707 horsepower and 645 lb-ft of torque.

The crown jewel of the lineup is the SRT flagship, which packs a whopping 777 horsepower and 707 lb-ft of torque.

In terms of suspension, the Rumble Bee trucks feature a unique setup that combines the benefits of on-road comfort and off-road capability.
