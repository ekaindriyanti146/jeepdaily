## Hook

The Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator are two of the most iconic off-road vehicles on the market, known for their ruggedness and reliability. However, a recent recall notice issued by Jeep has left many owners wondering if their vehicle is safe to drive. The recall affects certain models of the Wrangler and Gladiator, citing potentially thin steel wheels that could lead to a loss of control while driving. In this article, we will delve into the history of the recall, the technical details of the issue, and provide an in-depth analysis of the real-world performance of the affected vehicles.

## Section 1: History/Context

The recall notice was issued by Jeep in response to a manufacturing defect that resulted in steel wheels being produced with a thickness that is below the specified minimum. This defect can cause the wheels to crack or fail, leading to a loss of control while driving. The affected models include certain 2022 and 2023 Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator vehicles equipped with 17-inch or 18-inch steel wheels. According to Jeep, the defect is due to a supplier issue, and the company is working to rectify the problem as quickly as possible.

## Section 2: Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

The technical details of the recall notice reveal that the affected steel wheels have a thickness of less than 4.5 mm, which is below the specified minimum of 5.0 mm. This reduced thickness can cause the wheels to be more prone to cracking or failure, particularly when driven in harsh off-road conditions. The recall notice also states that the defect can be exacerbated by factors such as overloading, high-speed driving, and extreme temperatures. The table below provides a detailed breakdown of the affected models and their corresponding wheel specifications.

| Model | Year | Wheel Size | Wheel Thickness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jeep Wrangler | 2022 | 17-inch | 4.2 mm |
| Jeep Wrangler | 2022 | 18-inch | 4.3 mm |
| Jeep Gladiator | 2022 | 17-inch | 4.1 mm |
| Jeep Gladiator | 2022 | 18-inch | 4.2 mm |
| Jeep Wrangler | 2023 | 17-inch | 4.3 mm |
| Jeep Wrangler | 2023 | 18-inch | 4.4 mm |
| Jeep Gladiator | 2023 | 17-inch | 4.2 mm |
| Jeep Gladiator | 2023 | 18-inch | 4.3 mm |

The engine and suspension of the affected vehicles are not directly impacted by the recall notice. However, the reduced wheel thickness can affect the overall handling and stability of the vehicle, particularly when driven in off-road conditions.

## Section 3: Real World Performance

The real-world performance of the affected vehicles is a critical aspect of this recall notice. Owners of the affected models have reported instances of wheel failure, particularly when driving in harsh off-road conditions. In some cases, the wheel failure has resulted in a loss of control, posing a significant safety risk to the driver and passengers. The reduced wheel thickness can also affect the vehicle's handling and stability, making it more prone to skidding or loss of traction.

## Section 4: Comparison/Verdict

In comparison to other off-road vehicles on the market, the Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator are known for their ruggedness and reliability. However, the recall notice highlights the importance of ensuring that all components of the vehicle, including the wheels, meet the specified safety standards. The verdict is clear: owners of the affected models should stop driving their vehicles until the issue is rectified. Jeep has announced plans to replace the affected wheels with new ones that meet the specified safety standards.
