The Willys 392 features a robust suspension system, including heavy-duty shocks, springs, and stabilizer bars, which provide exceptional stability and control on uneven terrain.

The vehicle's four-wheel-drive system is equipped with a two-speed transfer case, allowing for seamless transitions between two-wheel and four-wheel drive.
