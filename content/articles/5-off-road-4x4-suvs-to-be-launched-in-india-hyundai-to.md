## Section 2: Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

The technical specifications of these upcoming SUVs are as varied as they are impressive. For instance, the Hyundai model is expected to come with a powerful diesel engine, paired with a robust 4×4 system for superior off-road capability. The Toyota offering, on the other hand, might feature a petrol engine with a sophisticated suspension system designed to absorb the shocks of rugged terrain. The table below provides a detailed comparison of the technical aspects of these SUVs:

| Vehicle | Engine Type | 4×4 System | Suspension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hyundai | Diesel | Part-time 4×4 | Coil Spring |
| Toyota | Petrol | Full-time 4×4 | Double Wishbone |
| Ford | Diesel | Part-time 4×4 | Leaf Spring |
| Mahindra | Diesel | Part-time 4×4 | Coil Spring |
| Isuzu | Diesel | Part-time 4×4 | Double Wishbone |

Each of these vehicles has been engineered to provide a unique driving experience, whether on paved roads or in the wilderness.

## Section 3: Real World Performance

The real-world performance of these SUVs is where their true mettle is tested. Off-road enthusiasts and adventure seekers will be keen to know how these vehicles handle challenging terrains, from steep inclines to water crossings. Early reviews and test drives suggest that each of these SUVs has its strengths and weaknesses. For example, the Hyundai model excels in its ability to navigate through dense forests and muddy paths, thanks to its advanced 4×4 system. Meanwhile, the Toyota SUV impresses with its stability and control on rocky and sandy dunes.

## Section 4: Comparison/Verdict

Comparing these off-road 4×4 SUVs is a complex task, given their diverse features and target markets. However, a key factor for many buyers will be the balance between off-road capability and on-road comfort. The verdict ultimately depends on individual preferences and needs. For those seeking a vehicle that can handle both daily commuting and weekend adventures, the Hyundai or Ford might be more appealing. On the other hand, buyers prioritizing pure off-road prowess might lean towards the Toyota or Mahindra.

## FAQs

1. **Q: Which of these SUVs offers the best off-road performance?**
A: The best off-road performance can vary based on the specific conditions and the driver's expertise. However, the Toyota model, with its full-time 4×4 system, is often touted for its exceptional capabilities in harsh terrains.

2. **Q: Are these SUVs suitable for on-road driving as well?**
A: Yes, all the mentioned SUVs are designed to provide a comfortable on-road experience. They come equipped with features like smooth transmissions, comfortable seating, and advanced infotainment systems.

3. **Q: What is the expected price range of these SUVs?**
A: The prices are expected to vary significantly, ranging from approximately ₹20 lakhs for the more affordable options to over ₹50 lakhs for the high-end models.

4. **Q: Will these SUVs be available in both petrol and diesel variants?**
A: While some models like the Hyundai will likely come in diesel, others such as the Toyota might be available in petrol. The availability of variants can depend on market demand and regulatory factors.
