## Technical Details of the Recall

The recall affects certain models of the Jeep Wrangler, specifically those with weak wheels that may fail under stress. The weak wheels are caused by a manufacturing defect, which can lead to a loss of control and increased risk of accidents. The technical details of the recall are as follows:

| **Component** | **Description** | **Failure Mode** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wheels | Weak wheels due to manufacturing defect | Failure under stress, leading to loss of control |
| Suspension | Affected by weak wheels, may contribute to loss of control | Reduced stability and handling |
| Engine | Unaffected by recall, but may be impacted by weak wheels | Reduced performance and efficiency |

The recall highlights the importance of proper manufacturing and quality control procedures. The weak wheels are a result of a defect in the production process, which can have serious consequences for drivers.
