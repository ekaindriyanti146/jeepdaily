## Section 1: History/Context

The Jeep Wrangler's history dates back to the 1940s, when the first Jeep, the Willys MB, was introduced as a military vehicle.

Over the years, the Jeep has undergone numerous transformations, with the Wrangler being introduced in 1986 as a more civilian-friendly version of the CJ series.

Since then, the Wrangler has become synonymous with off-road adventure, with its removable doors, fold-down windshield, and rugged design making it the perfect vehicle for exploring the great outdoors.

The Wrangler's popularity has endured for decades, with each new generation bringing improvements in design, technology, and capabilities.

## Section 2: Technical Details/Engine/Suspension

The 2026 Jeep Wrangler Rubicon is a testament to the brand's commitment to innovation and performance.

Under the hood, the Rubicon features a 3.6-liter V6 engine, producing 285 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque.

The engine is paired with an eight-speed automatic transmission, providing smooth and responsive shifting.

The Rubicon also features a robust suspension system, with a disconnecting front sway bar, allowing for improved articulation and stability on uneven terrain.

The vehicle's four-wheel-drive system, featuring a two-speed transfer case, provides unparalleled traction and control.

Other notable features include a 33.5-inch ground clearance, 10.8 inches of suspension travel, and a maximum towing capacity of 3,500 pounds.
